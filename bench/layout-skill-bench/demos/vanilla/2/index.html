features

No plugins to babysit. No cloud lock-in. Just notes that behave like a well-indexed codebase.

capture

Quick-capture everywhere

Global hotkey → thought saved. Clipboard, URL, voice, and screenshot all become searchable notes in under a second.

autolink

Graph, not folders

Every note gets wired to related notes automatically. Explore your thinking as a graph or a list — your call.

recall

Ask your past self

Hybrid semantic + keyword search over your entire history. Results cite exact lines, with diffs against your edits.

diff

Version-controlled

Every change is a commit you can browse, blame, and revert. Git under the hood, zero setup on the surface.

sync

E2E-encrypted sync

Device-to-device CRDT sync. Works offline for weeks, merges without conflicts, self-host if you want.

export

Plain text forever

Your vault is just Markdown on your disk. Open it in vim if you like. We'll wait. We're local-first, seriously.
