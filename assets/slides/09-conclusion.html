  • Keep the start of the prompt stable. A change there invalidates the cache for everything after it.
  • Add to the end. A fork, a compaction or an edit halfway costs 9 min at 512K; a new message at the end costs seconds.
  • Plan for the first load: about 3 min at 128K, 13 min at 512K. Then stay in the session.
