The Method

Capture.
Compost.
Bloom.

Three quiet habits, one notebook. No systems to maintain, no plugin garage — just the oldest writing technology there is: notes on notes.

One review, every Sunday · Twelve minutes

  1. I

    Capture in the margins

    Clip passages from articles, books, and podcasts as you meet them. They arrive already wearing their citations like bookmarks.

    clippingsauto-citations
  2. II

    Compost every Sunday

    A twelve-minute walk through what you saved. Most of it gets discarded, deliberately. What survives is actually yours.

    weekly reviewdeliberate forgetting
  3. III

    Bloom into essays

    Clusters of notes surface as drafts. The index writes itself; you write the part only you can write.

    draftsself-writing index
