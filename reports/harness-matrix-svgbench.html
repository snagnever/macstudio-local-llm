SVGBench is a question bank of 105 drawing prompts, each with a checklist of concrete requirements (pinned commit dd42e2c, July 2025). Seven harness-matrix prompts matched a question, so 84 SVGs were rendered in headless Chrome and graded strictly, one requirement at a time: half-met counts as failed, and anything the renderer doesn't show doesn't count. 413 of 794 requirement checks met. Every verdict on this board comes from a single judge, claude-opus-5. The board it replaces used four judges, two of them grading their own drawings — what that was worth is measured below. Different subset, route and judge make these results unsuitable for comparison with the upstream leaderboard.

What one judge changed

The previous board used four judges: a Fable model on most artifacts, and two models that graded their own drawings. Every artifact was re-judged by claude-opus-5. 43 artifacts appear on both boards, so their old and new verdicts can be compared directly.

The second take helped; animation never did

OpenCode × GPT-5.6 Terra generated v1 without prior artifacts, then used fresh agents for v2, v3, and an animated take. Under the single judge, v2 is the best take on three of the seven prompts and v3 on one; two prompts never move across all four takes, and the rubber ducky ends three requirements below its own v2. The animated take scores exactly what v3 scores on all seven prompts — SMIL motion neither adds nor loses points with a judge that reads a PNG.

The dolphin through a hula hoop — five of the six pairs drew it, 21 artifacts in all.
