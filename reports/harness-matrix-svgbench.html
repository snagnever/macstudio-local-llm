SVGBench is a question bank of 105 drawing prompts, each with a checklist of concrete requirements (pinned commit dd42e2c, July 2025). Seven harness-matrix prompts matched a question, so 43 SVGs were rendered in headless Chrome and graded strictly, one requirement at a time: half-met counts as failed, and anything the renderer doesn't show doesn't count. 176 of 407 requirement checks met. The newest 28 checks are self-judged by the model that drew them and are surfaced separately below. Different subset, route and judges make these results unsuitable for comparison with the upstream leaderboard.
