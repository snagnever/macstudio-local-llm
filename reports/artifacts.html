Comparing tasks between Qwen3.8 Flash Next and frontier models

This was made for fun, without much scientific rigour — the artifacts will show you that. Do not take it too seriously, but I think it shows a good model running locally, and at a very good speed.

Its drawings and its game sit next to what hosted frontier models made from the same prompts, and they hold up. Judge them by eye: the scores are in each section, and they say less than the artifacts do. Every picture is the model's own work — the SVG file it wrote, or a screenshot of the app it built. A stack is one harness running one model.

    SVG drawings, one prompt per row

    Each row heading is the verbatim prompt; each drawing is one stack's answer. The score is requirements met over requirements asked, judged by claude-opus-5 on the rendered drawing. Open a drawing to read every requirement with the judge's note. Last take is the drawing a stack ended on, preferring an animated one; best take is its highest-scoring drawing on that prompt. Take evolution lines up one stack's takes of one drawing in the order it made them, with the change in requirements met at each step.
