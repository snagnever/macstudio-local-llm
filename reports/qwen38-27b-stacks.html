Qwen3.8-27B stacks vs the Flash-Next driver

Three stacks for the dense 27B (mlx-dspark control, Splash 1.3.1, mlx-serve 26.10.2) measured with the same agent-turn probe as the two Qwen3.8-Flash-Next daily drivers: A, Swift 1.5 (w1, T_turno 7.9 / 8.6 s) and B, ddalcu iQ (n2, T_turno 8.1 / 8.9 s), both on mlx-serve 26.10.1 at 32K / 128K context.

Finding

Splash 1.3.1 is the fastest way to run the dense 27B on this rig: T_turno 17.7 s (xhigh) / 15.9 s (medium) at 128K, 0.69× the mlx-dspark control; still about 2× the Flash-Next driver (8.6 s), so the 27B stays a NO-GO as the agentic driver.

Setup and how to reproduce

Apple M4 Max, 128 GB. Every arm serves the dense Qwen3.8-27B; speed phases run each arm at xhigh and medium effort (file tags p<N>x / p<N>m), 3 repetitions, bands 32K then 128K. The control s27r ran medium at 32K only. The two Flash-Next references come from bench/swift15-flashnext-2026-10, both on mlx-serve 26.10.1 at xhigh effort: driver A (w1, Swift 1.5 MLX-4.7bpw) with T_turno 7.9 / 8.6 s and driver B (n2, ddalcu iQ-MLX-4.7bpw) with T_turno 8.1 / 8.9 s at 32K / 128K. The 1.25 × limit and the driver verdict use driver A only.
