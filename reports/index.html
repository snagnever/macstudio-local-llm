⚡ Decode speed collapses with model size

🔬 Hallucination experiment — thinking cuts false claims 54%

Same prompts with vs without thinking enabled, across 3 models × 3 runtimes × 2 temperatures (36 pairs of samples).

Thinking reduces hallucination by 53.6% (64.5% → 29.9% false claims). The gap only appears above 0.6B. On a single 4-bit quant the effect flips sign (-50.6%) — quantization quality matters as much as the thinking switch.
