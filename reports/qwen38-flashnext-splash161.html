Method and limits

  • One machine (Mac Studio M4 Max 128 GB), one model at a time, the same weights on both releases. Base flags --verify-rows auto --idle-release off --language-only --no-webui --offline.
  • Sampling: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, frequency_penalty 0, reasoning effort xhigh.
  • Every speed verdict compares with 1.5.0 in the same queue: the earlier campaign saw a 2× change in the summed decode of two 256K sessions between days.
  • Single stream: 3 boots per arm and band in ABBA order. Two sessions: 3 reps per arm. Claude Code: 2 reps on 1.5.0 and 3 on 1.6.1.
  • The 1M row, the quality passes of 1.5.0 and the 1.6.0 and 1.5.0 columns of Task 7 come from other days.
  • The 512K profile of the launcher was not measured on 1.6.1.
  • This page restates the stage reports; the Task 5 gaps and the Task 7 probes are computed from the committed JSONL by bench/splash161-2026-11/scripts/render_page.py.
